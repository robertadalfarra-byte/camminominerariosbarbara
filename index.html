Trip concept

This itinerary uses the official walking route, stages 1–6: Iglesias → Nebida → Masua → Buggerru → Portixeddu → Piscinas → Montevecchio. The official CMSB site currently describes the whole route as 500 km in 30 stages.

Important: the 2027 trip is far enough away that accommodation availability and prices cannot be confirmed yet. Treat the prices below as the latest published/indicative figures, not a booking confirmation. Re-check with each property before paying.

Planning assumptions

  • 4 people, willing to use dorm/shared rooms or two twin/double rooms.
  • Walking starts 26 May and finishes 31 May.
  • 25 May: airport → Iglesias and overnight.
  • 1 June: recovery/transfer day.
  • 2 June: Cagliari buffer night.
  • 3 June: Cagliari → CAG and flight home.

Lunch & dinner strategy

25 May · Iglesias

  • Dinner: Biki Biki Sisters, Bottega Red or Trattoria S'Arrastu — CMSB currently marks these with the pilgrim menu.
  • Buy for trail: water, fruit, panini, nuts, electrolyte sachets and breakfast top-ups.

26 May · Stage 1

  • Lunch: carry a packed lunch; the 20.4 km day is long.
  • Dinner: Su Mustazzeddu / La Piazzetta Bistrot in Nebida. Restaurant Oasi is another practical current option.

27 May · Stage 2

  • Lunch: Porto Flavia restaurant is the principal official trail-side option; Bar La Piazzetta is useful before/after leaving Nebida.
  • Dinner: Masua has very limited food supply; arrange dinner with the accommodation/Porto Flavia and do not rely on a supermarket.

28 May · Stage 3

  • Lunch/stop: Oasi Beach at Cala Domestica provides food and can also arrange transfers/activities; Warung Beach Club is another trail-area option.
  • Dinner: Buggerru — Bistrot Ristorante Galleria 13, La Baia da Tore, AQUA or another open local restaurant. Reserve in advance for a group.

29 May · Stage 4

  • Lunch: start with breakfast/packed food from Buggerru. Portixeddu's restaurants and beach kiosks are seasonal; May availability must be checked.
  • Dinner: Bar Ristorante L'Ancora or accommodation dinner/transfer. Keep a backup packed meal.

30 May · Stage 5

  • Lunch: pack food in Portixeddu. This is a remote stage and the final sandy/rocky section is tiring.
  • Dinner: if staying at La Quercia, arrange dinner there or by transfer; if staying at a hotel, use its restaurant. Do not assume restaurants at Piscinas are open in May.

31 May · Stage 6

  • Lunch: pack a substantial lunch and 3 L water/person minimum for the remote section.
  • Dinner: easiest in Guspini after a pre-arranged transfer. If staying at Montevecchio, pre-arrange dinner because evening choices are limited.

1–3 June · recovery / Cagliari

  • Use the buffer for a relaxed Sardinian dinner, shopping and laundry.
  • Keep 2 June in Cagliari to protect the 3 June flight.
Pilgrim menu: CMSB's Mìnera Card FAQ currently describes a €18 “Menu del Pellegrino” consisting of first or second course + side + drink + cover charge, where offered. Confirm the 2027 menu with the restaurant.

Transfers & logistics

CAG → Iglesias

Best for 4: pre-book a private minivan/driver, especially with hiking luggage. Iglesias Tourism states that Iglesias is about 40 minutes by road from Cagliari-Elmas. Public transport is possible via the airport rail station and onward connections.

Iglesias transport information →

Local trail transfers

CMSB lists local NCC/minivan operators and transfer services. Piscinas is particularly important: the official Stage 5 page says there are no public transport services at Piscinas, so a private transfer is needed for onward movement.

CMSB transport directory →

Stage 6 finish

Montevecchio is not the easiest place for a group dinner/overnight. Pre-book a driver to Guspini and use the extra 1 June as recovery. This is more robust than depending on a single small property in the mining village.

Public buses

ARST line 808 links Iglesias, Nebida and Masua. Timetables change by day type, so use the official live timetable rather than copying a 2026 schedule into the 2027 trip.

Cammino-ready packing list

  • Trail shoes with reliable grip; sandals optional for evenings.
  • Light rain shell + wind layer.
  • Sun hat, sunglasses and SPF.
  • 2–3 L water capacity per person; Stage 3 officially advises at least 3 L.
  • Electrolytes and high-calorie trail snacks.
  • Offline CMSB app/maps + downloaded GPX tracks.
  • Power bank; reception can disappear on Stage 3.
  • Light headlamp.
  • Microfibre towel / sleeping-bag liner for Posadas.
  • Small first-aid kit + blister treatment.
  • Swimwear and light beach layer.
  • Reusable food bag for packed lunches.
  • Credential; necessary for pilgrim accommodation rates.
  • Small amount of cash as a backup.
